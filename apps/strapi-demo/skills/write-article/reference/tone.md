# How Field Notes sounds

- Plain words: "use", not "utilize"; "help", not "facilitate".
- "We" for the Field Notes team is fine. Speak to the reader as "you".
- Specific beats general: numbers, names, places, the actual time a recipe takes.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no openers like "In today's fast-paced world".
- Dates in full, day first: 17 October 2026.
- Numbers one to nine in words, 10 and up in digits.
- American spelling: color, flavor, organize.
