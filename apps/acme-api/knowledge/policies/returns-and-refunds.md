# Returns and refunds

Customers can return unused items in their original packaging within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund. Tents, sleeping bags and packs that were set up indoors to try them count as unused. Worn footwear and used climbing gear can't be returned, unless they are faulty (see [Warranty](warranty.md)).

## How a return works

1. Create a return label with the carrier (`carrier_create_return_label`). Labels are free for faulty, damaged or wrong items, and $7.95 otherwise, taken off the refund.
2. Tell the customer to pack the item and drop it off within 14 days.
3. Refund once the parcel is scanned by the carrier. Don't wait for it to arrive.

## Damaged on arrival

When an item arrives damaged (a torn rainfly, a bent pole, a crushed box with broken contents), ask for a photo if the customer hasn't sent one, then offer the customer the choice:

- **A replacement**, shipped express at no cost. For tents, replace only the damaged part when we stock it (see the parts fitment table) instead of the whole tent: it arrives sooner and the customer keeps the rest.
- **A full refund**, with a free return label for the damaged item.

Customers don't have to send the damaged item back first. If they are about to leave on a trip, ship the replacement the same day and send the return label with it.

Minor cosmetic damage that doesn't affect use (a scuffed stuff sack, a dented pot lid) can be settled with a partial refund of 15 to 25% if the customer would rather keep the item.

## Wrong size and exchanges

The first exchange for a different size is free, label included. Ship the new size as soon as the return is scanned. Further exchanges are treated as a return and a new order.

## Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method and show up within 5 to 7 business days. The customer gets an automatic email.

Refund limits for staff:

| Amount | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Up to $25 | Anyone, no approval |
| Up to $150 | Support staff, with approval |
| Up to $1,000 | Admins |
| Over $1,000 | Finance, through a finance ticket |

## Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they ship. Once shipped, treat a cancellation as a return.
