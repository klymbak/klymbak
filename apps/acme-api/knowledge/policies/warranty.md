# Warranty

Everything we sell has a **two-year warranty** against defects in materials and workmanship, from the delivery date. Customers need their order number; no receipt is required.

## What it covers

- Zippers that split, lose teeth or won't close
- Seams that open or leak, and delaminating seam tape
- Tent poles that break or bend in normal use
- Buckles, straps and hip belts that break
- Stoves, filters and headlamps that stop working

## Repair or replace

- **In the first 90 days**, replace the item or the faulty part, shipped express, at no cost. Customers don't have to send the faulty item back first.
- **After 90 days**, repair it: send a free label to our repair partner, Northpeak Repair Center in Boulder. Repairs take about two weeks, and the item comes back by express. If it can't be repaired, replace it, or refund it if we no longer sell it.

A broken pole section is replaced with a new pole set at any time during the warranty, since pole sets are cheaper to ship than a repair.

## What isn't covered

Wear and tear (worn soles, faded fabric), damage from misuse (a tent pitched in winds above its rating, a pack overloaded past 25 kg), animal damage and gear modified by the customer. For these, offer a paid repair through Northpeak Repair Center or a 15% discount on a replacement.
