# Shipping and delivery

We ship to the US, Canada, the UK, Germany, France, the Netherlands, Sweden and Australia with UPS, FedEx, DHL and USPS. Orders placed before 2 pm Mountain Time on a business day leave the warehouse the same day.

## Delivery times

| Destination | Standard | Express |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US | 3 to 5 business days | 1 to 2 business days |
| Canada | 5 to 8 business days | 2 to 3 business days |
| UK and EU | 5 to 9 business days | 2 to 4 business days |
| Australia | 7 to 12 business days | 4 to 6 business days |

Shipping is free on orders over $75 in the US and over $150 elsewhere. Duties and taxes for the UK and the EU are included in the price, so customers never pay on delivery.

## Late or stuck parcels

A parcel is **stuck** when its tracking hasn't changed for 4 business days.

1. Check the tracking with the carrier (`carrier_track_shipment`). If the carrier reports a delay with a new date, tell the customer the new date.
2. Otherwise ask the carrier for a trace and tell the customer we did.
3. If it's still stuck 7 business days after the last scan, consider it lost: reship the order at no cost, by express, or refund it in full, whichever the customer prefers. Don't wait for the carrier's claim to settle.

When the customer needs the item by a date (a trip, a race), offer an express reship right away instead of waiting for the trace.

## Delivered but not received

Ask the customer to check with neighbours and their building's mailroom, then wait 2 business days. After that, treat it as lost and reship or refund.

## Missing items

When a parcel arrives without one of the items, check the order's items and ship the missing one by express. No return is needed.
