### Care

- Never store a tent wet: dry it within 24 hours, or mildew stains the fabric and smells. Mildew isn't covered by the warranty.
- Clean with lukewarm water and a sponge. No detergent or washing machine: it strips the waterproof coating.
- The seams are factory taped. After about three seasons, re-seal them with seam sealer.

### Common questions

**Is the rainfly waterproof?** Yes, rated 3000 mm on the Ridgeline tents, enough for sustained heavy rain. Condensation inside on cold nights is normal, not a leak: open both vents.

**Can I replace just the rainfly?** Yes, rainflies, footprints and pole sets are sold as spare parts.

## Sleeping bags

| Bag | Comfort | Limit | Fill |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alpine 20°F Down Bag | 28°F (-2°C) | 20°F (-7°C) | 800-fill down |
| Trailhead 35°F Synthetic Bag | 42°F (6°C) | 35°F (2°C) | Synthetic |

The number in the name is the **limit** rating, where a sleeper stays safe but not warm. Customers who sleep cold should go by the comfort rating, and a pad matters as much as the bag: the Foam Trail Pad adds little insulation on cold ground, the Cloudrest Inflatable Pad more.

Down bags can be washed in a front-loading machine with down wash, then tumble-dried on low with two tennis balls. Store them loose, not in the stuff sack.
