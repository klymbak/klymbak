# e-Ukraine

The Ukrainian pages set their text in e-Ukraine and their headings in e-Ukraine Head, the typefaces of [Diia](https://diia.gov.ua/).

- Designed by Dmitry Rastvortsev for the Ministry of Digital Transformation of Ukraine.
- Source: https://thedigital.gov.ua/fonts (e-Ukraine.zip and e-Ukraine-Head.zip, OTF).
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), the license of all content on thedigital.gov.ua.
- Changes: converted from OTF to WOFF2 with fontTools; glyphs and metadata are unchanged.

Only the faces the site uses are here: e-Ukraine Regular, Medium and Bold, and e-Ukraine Head Bold.
